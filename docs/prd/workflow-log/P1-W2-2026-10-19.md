### Review rounds (what they caught)

- **A03, round 1 → changes_required.** Four integrity defects, all real: `class_group`'s natural key omitted `campus_id` (two campuses could not reuse a stream name); `mark`'s unique key included nullable `component_id`, a no-op under SQLite's NULL-is-distinct rule; `learning_area_enrolment`'s unique key collapsed effective-dated history; `fee_category_ids` had no registered target entity. The fix added campus scope to `class_group`/`teaching_group`/`fee_schedule`, `UniqueKey { columns, where }` partial-key metadata for NULL-component marks, effective-dated `learning_area_enrolment` uniqueness, and the `fee_category` entity (63rd entity) with a registry test over every `*_ids` array. Round 2: **approve**, each fix re-proven against a real `node:sqlite` database generated from the metadata.
- **D01, round 1 → changes_required.** The empty-state CTA was keyboard-unreachable: Tab on the open trigger closed the popup and unmounted the CTA (WCAG 2.1.1, and one of the five acceptance behaviours). The fix keeps the popup mounted while focus moves into the CTA and closes from a root `focusout`, with Escape restoring focus to the trigger; a populated list still closes on Tab. Also gated the empty state on a settled lookup, added `role=status`/`aria-live`, made archived values clearable without the `allowClear` opt-in, and covered live creation. Round 2: **approve**.
