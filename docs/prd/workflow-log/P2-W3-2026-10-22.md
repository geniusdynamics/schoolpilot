## Findings worth carrying forward

1. **The pack contract revision was worth doing once.** Five packs independently invented the same
   workarounds: `A_STAR` for `A*`, a summative slot for an observational scheme, a single calendar
   template for two British years, and no way to say "at least one humanity". All thirteen gaps are
   now closed in the schema and the loader, and the shipped packs use the real printed symbols.

2. **Q01 paid for itself on its first run.** 9 of 58 vectors were being skipped, and both causes were
   genuine bugs, not test noise: three British packs declared `sum_points` for what their own vectors
   expect to be a plain total (spec 03 defines `sum_points` as aggregate-then-lookup), and B04
   collapsed `else: repeat_or_council` into `repeat`, taking a decision the council owns. Fixed in
   Q01a. The runner reports skips with reasons, so this cannot silently drift again.

3. **A page can be built, tested, gated and still unreachable.** E02 and E03 were both complete and
   green with no route, because route registration lives outside a page's ownership. Worse, the
   obvious fix — adding a navigation entry — does nothing: `DynamicRoutes` reads the module's
   separate `routes` array. Both are now wired and covered by the routing suite (85 → 87 cases).

4. **Three flaky tests were fixed rather than tolerated:** the setup-wizard suite (Testing Library's
   1 s `waitFor` default on a suite that drives the whole wizard through the real provider), the
   report print e2e (cold Vite compile exceeding the 30 s navigation default), and the conformance
   runner self-test (a cold child-process spawn exceeding vitest's 20 s default).

5. **Two agents were cancelled mid-flight.** B04 and D02 had complete, uncommitted work; it was
   salvaged, gated and integrated rather than restarted. B03's follow-up was genuinely half-done and
   was resumed. One integration error was caught this way: A01a's follow-up commit had been made but
   never merged, so W3 still lacked four fields when B03's follow-up looked for them.

## Known gaps carried out of this wave

- Per-group assessment workflow and moderation history have **no canonical entity**; E01 keeps them
  in a durable browser store behind an interface, so they are per-device until a schema entity exists.
- **The pack registry is not wired into the UI.** E01 resolves schemes from `assessment_scheme` rows
  and E02 reads the pack JSON directly; E03 takes its aggregation definitions as a prop.
- `competency_observation` has no `academic_period_id`, so observations summarise by class and
  learning area rather than by term, and the derived term judgement is displayed but not persisted.
- `src/styles/forms.css` still declares a global `@page { margin: 1in; size: A4 }` that fights the
  per-template print geometry DR-05 requires.
- The canonical `grading_scale` vocabulary still says `mark_out_of_20` where the engine says
  `out_of_20`, and a canonical `learning_area` row carries no coefficient or credit.
